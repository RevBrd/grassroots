# Grassroots

**[Grow your first post →](https://revbrd.github.io/grassroots/)**

*Organic reach. Grown in a lab.*

The algorithm hates ads. It loves stories. So Grassroots turns your ad into a story: a rant, a
genuine question, a humble milestone, or five things you learned getting laughed out of a coding
interview. Then it mentions your product in paragraph four.

## Features

- **Seven proven formats.** Proven by us, on a dashboard you can't see.
- **Four placements**, including "in an EDIT nobody asked for" and "in the comments, from
  another account."
- **Vulnerability and Subtlety dials.** From LinkedIn to Therapy, and from Billboard to Spy.
- **Seed comments** for your other accounts, ready to copy.
- **Authenticity™**, our proprietary metric.
- **Detectability**, the other one.
- **One human typo, on request.** Mistakes by [MaaS™](https://revbrd.github.io/maas/), a
  Grassroots partner.
